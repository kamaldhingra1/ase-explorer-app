Executive Overview

What is an AI Agent?

An AI agent is software that can interpret goals, reason over context, use tools, remember information, and take actions with varying levels of autonomy.

Why SATF Matters

SATF gives business and technology leaders a shared way to discuss autonomy, oversight, trust boundaries, control points, and business impact.
