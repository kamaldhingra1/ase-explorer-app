Executive Track

Leadership questions for trusted autonomy

What leaders need to understand

Agentic systems are not just chat interfaces. They can plan, call tools, remember context, and trigger business workflows.

Key leadership questions

What can the agent do? Who granted its authority? What data can it access? What outbound channels can it use? What evidence is retained?

Business outcomes to monitor

Operational disruption, financial loss, regulatory exposure, reputation damage, and safety-sensitive downstream effects.
